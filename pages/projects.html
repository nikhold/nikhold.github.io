Doomsday Algorithm & Scopone Scientifico Programs

  • Context: ECE 209 (Computer Systems Programming) | Timeline: Spring 2026
  • Languages & Tools: C, CMake, CLion IDE
  • Summary: Programmed a Doomsday Algorithm to determine day of the week for any date and a Scopone Scientifico simulator, an Italian card game played with multi-leveled rules

The objective of these projects were to improve my understanding and application of C and to utilize differing data structures for different tasks. The programs are:

  • The Doomsday Algorithm: Introduction to basic C syntax and arithmetic; calculates the day of the week of a given date from the user, which can be any date between 1583 and 2299.
  • Scopone Scientifico: Utilizes multi-file I/O and advanced conditional structures; simulates the card game of the same name between "players", and navigating complicated rules to determine the winner. The variation in the game from changing the order of the cards entered from the user.
